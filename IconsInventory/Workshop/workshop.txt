description=[h3]🖌️ Selection painting[/h3]

description=[h3]🎮 Controller bindings[/h3]
description=The D-Pad alone can move you through both player and loot windows naturally. However I felt that having a quicker way to switch windows was necessary.
description=
description=So in current design, LB/L1 and RB/R1 have been supercharged:
description=[list]
description=[*]Press and hold + Up/Down to navigate containers
description=[*]Press and hold + Left/Right to switch focus to the other window
description=[*]Tapping is not changed (cycle containers or whatever vanilla decides)
description=[/list]
description=In any case I'm very interested in your feedback, especially if you play on controller!
